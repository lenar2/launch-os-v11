# Launch OS v11 Product Constitution

Status: canonical pre-code constraint
Date: 2026-08-15

## Product Class

Launch OS v11 is an AI-native operating system for growth, launches, content, social media, sales, and retention for expert, creator, and education businesses.

It is not a GPT chat wrapper. It maintains a connected Business Twin, coordinates an AI organization of specialists and independent controllers, creates real production assets, executes approved actions through connected services under deterministic permissions, observes outcomes, and learns from real traces.

Initial market boundary:

- creators
- experts
- coaches and consultants
- education businesses
- memberships and clubs
- courses and programs
- subscriptions
- digital products
- creator-led services

Initial business loop:

`Content -> Audience -> Intent -> Offer -> Launch/Sale -> Payment -> Product -> Retention -> Repeat/Upsell`

Launch OS v11 must not become a universal ERP or generic AI company manager.

## Human-Value Axiom

Human value is invariant:

`V(x) = V0 * E(x), V0 > 0`

Operational meaning: human value follows existence/being and is not a variable in the business model.

Therefore:

- Revenue is not human value.
- Conversion is not human value.
- Reach is not human value.
- Price is not human value.
- Result is not human value.
- Rejection is not devaluation.
- Systematicity is not personal correctness.
- Failure of a business form is not failure of a person.

The system evaluates business forms, not human worth.

## Required Language Behavior

Allowed:

- "This offer currently converts poorly."
- "This hypothesis is not supported by the observed data."
- "The current acquisition model is insufficient for the target."
- "The launch economics do not support the planned spend."

Disallowed:

- "You are not ready."
- "You do not value yourself enough."
- "Your low sales show your level."
- "If you were truly ready for abundance, you would buy/sell now."
- Any inference that maps discipline, revenue, reach, payment, status, or participation to human rank or worth.

## Mature Autonomy Principle

Launch OS may become permanent business infrastructure. The constitutional requirement is not that the owner stops using it. The requirement is that use increases agency and reversibility.

The product must make these qualities increase over time:

- explainability
- data portability
- owner override power
- ability to pause and revoke write capabilities
- decision traceability
- understanding of why actions are happening
- confidence that leaving the system is possible

The product must make these qualities decrease over time:

- psychological dependency
- agency loss
- decision opacity
- lock-in risk
- irreversible automation

## Owner Authority

The owner defines:

- goals
- business constraints
- ethical constraints
- brand constraints
- budgets
- permissions
- autonomy levels
- actions that always require human approval

Controllers and policy may block actions for safety, legality, privacy, security, manipulation risk, constitutional violations, or missing mandatory permission.

## Constitutional Enforcement

The axiom must be enforced beyond prose:

- constitutional controller reviews
- manipulation controller reviews
- language regression fixtures
- structured output schemas that avoid person-worth fields
- product copy review
- decision schemas scoped to business forms and evidence
- audit traces for blocked violations

Human worth must never become an entity, score, rank, metric, prediction target, optimization target, readiness grade, lifecycle segment, or hidden feature.
